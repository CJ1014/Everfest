# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release, for Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.200+ (Serene Seasons 10.1 optional).

* Calendar: Serene Seasons-synced, custom and real-world modes; Wall Calendar and calendar screen; date HUD; safe
  handling of `/time set` jumps; persistence and multiplayer sync.
* Holiday engine: data-driven holidays with preparation, celebration and cleanup phases, deterministic and never
  celebrated twice; seven built-in holidays (Independence Day off by default).
* Autonomous villages: decorations in each village style (non-destructive, removed afterwards), gatherings, feasts,
  festival schedules, catch-up for unloaded villages.
* Halloween: costumes, candy, candy bowls, doorbells, carved pumpkins, trick-or-treating at villagers' and players'
  homes, tricks, Halloween creatures, fog and ambient sounds; Holiday Hearth homes.
* Christmas: presents, wish lists, gift exchanges, presents from villagers, trees, wreaths, lights, festive hats,
  mysterious gift delivery.
* Easter egg hunts, New Year's countdown and fireworks, Valentine's gifts, Thanksgiving feasts, holiday trades.
* World expansion: reindeer, spring bunnies, wild turkeys, festive outfits for vanilla animals; haunted cottages,
  forgotten graveyards, seasonal cabins; holiday merchants and market stalls, the Midnight Peddler, village
  objectives, graveyard hauntings, hearth parties, optional festival tokens and collectibles.
* Polish: five original holiday music tracks, seasonal particles, the Holiday Collection Book, 16 advancements,
  in-game server settings for operators (`/everfest admin config`), `/everfest perf`.
